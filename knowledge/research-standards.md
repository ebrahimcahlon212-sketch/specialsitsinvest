# Research standards

Earlier investor feedback is not available in this checkout. No rules below are attributed to that feedback. These standards come from AGENTS.md and the approved system specification. Build review findings belong in docs/build/.

- Re-read primary documents and cite their PDF file page numbers or text line locators. Inspect table images before using their figures.
- Keep sourced facts separate from labelled judgements. Missing evidence is a gap.
- Compute results from identified inputs in code and recompute published metrics.
- Respect investor account and tax constraints. Never place trades.
- Use structured FDA action or letter dates and SEC 8-K acceptance times under amendment 8. Prose can flag contradictions but cannot establish those dates.
- Research steps remain exempt from evidence gates. Legacy publication checks warn. New publication commands fail closed.
