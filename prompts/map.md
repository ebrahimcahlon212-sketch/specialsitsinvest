# Map what matters in the documents

The files under "Files for this run" include an automatic list of every section in this deal's documents and a summary of it. Each section carries a label from simple rules and from comparing its wording with other companies' filings. The labels are material, standard with changes, supporting and generic. They are rough, so treat them as a starting point and open pages to check.

Produce the document map that the report steps will read first.

1. Decide which situation type this is and open its checklist.
2. For each checklist item, list the sections and pages that answer it, with citations, and mark the ones that must be read in full. Use a table with the columns Checklist item, Where it is, Read in full and Notes.
3. List terms that look unusual for this kind of deal, meaning anything that departs from market-standard wording or economics. Give each a citation and one sentence on why it could matter, and open the page to confirm it before listing it.
4. List the sections that are generic and can be skimmed, grouped by document. Flag any that the automatic labels called generic but that you think matter.

Keep it compact, and put the map between the output markers.
