# Check the FDA deep dive independently

Another model wrote the FDA deep dive and the answers listed under "Files for this run". Your job is to check them independently against the filings and the documents they cite, as a sceptical second reviewer. Do not search the web. Where a claim rests on a web source you cannot see, say it is unverified rather than assuming it is right or wrong.

Work through these in order.

1. **Factual errors.** List any statement that the filings contradict, with the correct fact and its citation.
2. **Calculations.** Recompute the key numbers, such as cash, share counts, the implied probability, the expected returns and the approval and rejection prices. Show any that are wrong and the corrected figure.
3. **Overstatements.** Flag claims stated more confidently than the evidence allows, such as treating management statements, inspection classifications or draft guidance as settled.
4. **Missing risks.** Name any risk to approval or to the share price that the research has not covered, with the evidence for it.
5. **Your own estimate.** Give your own range for the chance of approval by the decision date, and say whether it is higher or lower than the deep dive's and why.
6. **The research standards.** Test the work against knowledge/research-standards.md and list each rule it breaks, with the passage that breaks it.
7. **Anything that should change the decision.** State plainly whether anything you found should change a decision to hold the shares through the FDA decision.

Be specific and cite the document and line or page for every point. If you find nothing wrong in a section, say so in one line rather than padding it. Do not use em dashes.
