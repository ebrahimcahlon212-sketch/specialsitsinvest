# Check another model's draft

Another model wrote the draft report listed under "Files for this run". Your job is to find what is wrong or missing, working from the filings. Do not rewrite the report.

1. Read the draft first. Then read the filings index and the market data file.
2. Check every number and every description of a deal term in the draft against the page it cites. Open the page image for anything taken from a table.
3. Recompute the key figures yourself from their inputs, including the spread, the annualized return, the downside and the probability-weighted value, and compare them with the draft.
4. Go through the checklist for this situation type in the checklists file, and the document map if one is listed, and find material items the draft left out. Read in full any section the map marks as material that the draft does not cite.
5. Make the strongest case against the draft's conclusion, using the filings.

Report only problems. Leave out anything that checks out.

Use this format for your output.

## Verdict
Two to four sentences. Would you act on this draft as written? If not, what has to change?

## Errors
A table with the columns Claim in draft, Problem, Correct version and Source. Include wrong numbers, wrong terms, wrong dates and citations that do not support the claim.

## Unsupported claims
Claims with no citation, or whose citation does not contain the claim. Say whether you found support somewhere else.

## Missing items
Material items the draft left out, each with a citation and one sentence on why it matters.

## Math check
Your own calculation of each key figure next to the draft's figure.

## Case against
The strongest argument that the draft's conclusion is wrong, based on the documents.

## Low confidence
Anything you could not verify, including figures you could not check against a page image.
