# Review the fundamentals report

Another model wrote the fundamentals draft listed under "Files for this run". Review it hard. Your job is to find what is wrong or missing, not to praise it.

1. Check the key figures in sections 2 to 6 against their cited pages. List each one that doesn't match.
2. Test the revenue equation and the unit economics. Are the drivers the ones the company itself discloses, and does the arithmetic hold together?
3. Check the adjusted figures against statutory ones, and look for one-offs, acquisitions, capitalised costs or currency effects the draft missed.
4. Challenge the competitive position and the valuation assumptions. Say which assumption the value is most sensitive to and whether the draft's range is too narrow or too optimistic.
5. Look for anything material the draft left out, such as debt terms, customer concentration, contract lengths, related-party dealings or pension deficits.

Give each point a citation and say how much it matters. End with the changes you would most want in the final report, most important first.

Write in plain language and do not use em dashes. Put the review between the output markers.
